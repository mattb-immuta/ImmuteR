### Meeting Notes
### Shell Account Status and Existing Immuta Usage
- Shell has been an enterprise customer for at least two to three years.
- Immuta was originally brought in to govern Shell’s data lake during centralization into the Enterprise Data Platform.
- The Enterprise Data Platform runs on Databricks across both AWS and Azure.
- Initial Immuta use cases centered on complex attribute-based access controls.
- HR became a flagship use case due to its complexity:
  - User metadata is sourced from Workday.
  - Metadata from the identity provider and Workday drives policies.
- Another active use case is data quality (DQ).
- The two acronyms currently in use are HRDP and DQ.
- Shell renewed this year and is not planning an immediate exit from Immuta.
- However, Shell’s stated intent is to reduce reliance on Immuta over time.
### Shell’s Databricks-First Strategy and Risk to Immuta
- Shell is fully committing to Databricks as its strategic platform.
- The company is highly cost-conscious and pushing to consolidate on fewer technologies.
- Leadership has directed teams to use Databricks first.
- Immuta is considered only when Databricks cannot meet required capabilities.
- Some stakeholders acknowledge gaps in Databricks today.
- They are nevertheless willing to wait for Databricks to deliver, pushing it via the product roadmap.
- New lines of business have stalled due to this Databricks-first direction.
- The group agreed that further persuasion of the current IT/platform team is unlikely to change the outcome.
- Conclusion: The top strategic priority is to find a new buyer with a differentiated pain point that Databricks does not solve.
### Marketplace, Collibra, and Data Product Strategy
- Shell previously built a version-one data product marketplace.
- The marketplace combined:
  - Collibra as the front end.
  - Immuta for access provisioning.
  - Databricks as the underlying data platform.
- Shell disliked governance users moving from Collibra into Immuta, even for limited screens.
- Shell later engineered around Immuta, connecting Collibra directly to Databricks.
- At that time, many data products were expected to be Delta Shares, and Immuta did not support Delta Shares.
- Immuta is currently not part of Shell’s marketplace journey.
- Marketplace adoption within Shell has been limited.
- There are questions about Collibra’s cost and value.
- Collibra remains due to sunk cost and internal sponsors.
### Organizational Challenges Inside Shell

- The CDAIO role was removed in the latest change.
- There is no centralized data governance function across the organization.
- Each line of business or platform team handles data governance independently.
- EDP is operated more like a technology function than a horizontal governance function.
- Attempts to position stronger governance controls have not succeeded.
- Existing contacts, including Royal and Sangamesh, are friendly but lack budget or influence to expand Immuta beyond EDP.
